Frequently Asked Questions

Integration, Security & Implementation

Key answers regarding how MAG Healthcare Solutions integrates with your existing hospital systems (HIS/EMR), security compliance, and deployment timeline.

How do MAG solutions connect and integrate with our existing Hospital Information System (HIS) or EMR?

We use a non-invasive, plug-and-play integration architecture designed to operate smoothly with any major commercial or custom Hospital Information System (HIS / EMR). We do not disrupt your existing clinical workflows or require replacement of your current infrastructure.

Our solutions support standard healthcare communication protocols and secure connection layers:

  • HL7 v2 & v3 Messaging: Direct ingestion of ADT (Admit/Discharge/Transfer), DFT (Detailed Financial Transactions), and SIU (Scheduling) message streams.
  • FHIR R4 RESTful APIs: Standardized modern JSON APIs for clinical resource querying and real-time KPI aggregation.
  • Direct Database Read-Replicas / SQL Connectors: Secure, read-only connections to MS SQL, Oracle, PostgreSQL, or MySQL data instances with zero write-access to primary hospital databases.
  • Automated Secure File Ingestion (SFTP / S3): Encrypted batch delivery of scheduled 837/835 claim remittance files, billing logs, and pharmacy inventory snapshots.
Epic Systems Cerner / Oracle Health Dedalus InterSystems TrakCare SAP Healthcare Custom HIS / EHR
What patient data privacy standards, encryption, and compliance levels are enforced?

Data governance and patient confidentiality are the foundation of our engineering practices. We comply strictly with HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act), UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018, and regional data protection regulations across the Middle East (including Saudi NDMO, CBAHI, and Egyptian Data Protection Law No. 151).

Our security controls include:

  • Source-Side PHI De-identification: Patient names, national IDs, and direct identifiers are stripped or pseudonymized before analytical processing or cloud transport.
  • End-to-End Encryption: Industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit for all data packets.
  • Zero-PHI Persistence Policy: Analytical pipelines process metrics on-the-fly without long-term unencrypted caching of identifiable medical records.
  • Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular permissions ensuring clinical, billing, and executive personnel access only authorized KPI views.
HIPAA Validated UK GDPR Compliant AES-256 / TLS 1.3 NDMO / CBAHI Aligned SOC 2 Principles
Do we need to sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) or Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)?

Yes, absolutely. Before any technical assessment, data intake, or pipeline connection takes place, MAG Healthcare Solutions executes a legally binding bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and, where applicable, a formal Business Associate Agreement (BAA) or Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

These legal agreements formally guarantee that all data shared remains confidential, is accessed solely within the agreed project boundaries, and is completely purged upon completion of the engagement unless a recurring service contract specifies otherwise.

What are the IT technical requirements and how long does deployment take?

We offer two flexible deployment architectures based on your hospital's IT policies:

  • Enterprise Cloud (SaaS / Dedicated Instance): Hosted on secure, compliant cloud infrastructure (AWS, Microsoft Azure, or National Government Cloud regions) requiring zero on-premise hardware.
  • On-Premise / Private Hospital Network: Deployed entirely inside your hospital's private intranet and firewalls for total data isolation.

Implementation Timeline: Initial RCM denial audit and data mapping is completed within 7 to 10 business days. Full live interactive dashboard deployment, automated ETL pipelines, and clinical team training typically take 2 to 4 weeks with minimal technical demand on your internal IT staff.

How are denial rules and dashboards customized for our local insurance payers and medical coding?

Every healthcare market has unique regulatory frameworks, payer contracts, and claims submission standards. Our engine is tailored specifically to your operational footprint:

  • Regional Payer Guidelines: Pre-configured rule sets for NHS CCGs/Trusts (UK), CCHI & NPHIES (Saudi Arabia), DHA & DOH/Shafafiya (UAE), and national private health insurance carriers.
  • Medical Coding Standards: Native support for ICD-10-AM/CM, CPT, HCPCS, ACHI, CDT, and DRG (Diagnosis-Related Group) classification structures.
  • Custom Contract Fee Schedules: Audit models cross-check approved service prices, co-pays, deductibles, and pre-authorization validity against your exact commercial contracts.
What ongoing support, maintenance, and training do you provide post-go-live?

We consider deployment just the starting point of our partnership. Our ongoing engagement model includes:

  • Dedicated RCM & Analytics Consultant: Continuous monthly reviews of claim rejection trends and cash collection improvements.
  • Staff Training & Upskilling: Practical, role-tailored workshops for medical billing clerks, clinical coders, and department supervisors.
  • Continuous System Monitoring & SLAs: Guaranteed uptime and response times outlined in our Service Level Agreement (SLA).
  • Quarterly Model Calibration: Regular updates to predictive denial models and demand forecasting algorithms to adapt to evolving payer policies.
